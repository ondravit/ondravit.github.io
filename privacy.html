Privacy Policy – AthleteLab Uploader

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What this app is

AthleteLab Uploader is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the AthleteLab channel. It is used only by that owner, to upload AthleteLab's own original videos to the AthleteLab YouTube channel and to manage those uploads (set titles, descriptions, privacy status and thumbnails). It is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.

Data the app accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, the app accesses only the AthleteLab YouTube channel that authorised it, in order to upload, update and delete that channel's own videos. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other users, and it does not access any other Google account data.

How data is stored

The OAuth credentials that let the app act on the channel are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used only by the owner's automated upload jobs. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

AthleteLab Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
